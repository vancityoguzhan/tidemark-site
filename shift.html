The OR-Tools Core

Shift utilizes the Google OR-Tools CP-SAT solver to navigate millions of possible staffing combinations. The engine treats scheduling as a mathematical optimization problem, balancing unbreakable labor laws against a ranked hierarchy of operational and employee penalties.

Hard Constraints (Unbreakable)
  • check_circle BC Labor Law: Max 5 consecutive days.
  • check_circle No "Clopens": PM/GY to AM transitions forbidden.
  • check_circle Role Integrity: Verified senior coverage per shift.

Development History

THE EVOLUTION
OF THE
SOLVER.

Tidemark wasn't designed in a vacuum. Each version was a direct response to a real failure: in privacy, in reliability, in trust. This is the record of how it grew.

v1.0 Foundation

The Logic Root.

First solvable week. 5 core roles. Single-pass extraction. The version that answered the only question that mattered: can a mathematical engine solve a hotel week?

v2.5 Privacy Rebuilt

Stability Hints.

Privacy layer rebuilt from pattern matching to structural NLP. Identity maps moved entirely to volatile RAM: no disk writes at any stage. The shift from "does it work?" to "is it safe enough to trust with real guest data?"

v5.5 Current

The Compliance Engine.

Neural model (BERT-Small ONNX) added alongside structural NLP and statistical filtering: all three must agree before a name is redacted. iOS native app. PDF and DOCX export. The version that ships.
