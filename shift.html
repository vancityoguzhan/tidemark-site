The OR-Tools Core

Shift utilizes the Google OR-Tools CP-SAT solver to navigate millions of possible staffing combinations. The engine treats scheduling as a mathematical optimization problem, balancing unbreakable labor laws against a ranked hierarchy of operational and employee penalties.

Hard Constraints
  • check_circle BC Labor Law: Max 5 consecutive days.
  • check_circle No "Clopens": PM/GY to AM transitions forbidden.
Soft Constraints
  • info Staffing Shortages (100,000 penalty).
  • info Shift Preferences (200 penalty).
visibility

Vision Extraction

Converting handwritten shift requests into structured JSON. Gemini Vision identifies staff IDs, dates, and intent from mobile-scanned request slips.

star

Stability Hints

The solver uses "stability hints" to maintain existing shifts while re-solving for new requirements, minimizing schedule disruption for your team.

BC Labor Law

THE LAW IS THE
LOWER BOUND.

calendar_today SEC. 36

Consecutive Days

The solver enforces a hard limit of 5 consecutive workdays. Any shift crossing this boundary triggers an immediate overtime penalty or is blocked by the CP-SAT engine.

history SEC. 32

The 32-Hour Rest

Shift tracks the 7-day rolling window across weekly snapshots to ensure every employee receives at least 32 consecutive hours of rest after their work week.

block HEALTH & SAFETY

No "Clopens"

The model forbids shift transitions that provide less than 8 hours of rest, specifically blocking PM-to-AM or Graveyard-to-AM transitions.
