# Fresh Tidemark concept

Active root route: `/` (rendered by `FreshExperience` in `src/main.tsx`). Audience: hotel operations managers preparing a weekly schedule. Primary action: request a personal walkthrough. Proof: interactive coded product views using fictional data and the operator's documented workflow; no invented metrics, customer logos, or security certifications.

## Direction before implementation

A clear view of the working week. Make the weekly roster the signature visual rather than borrowing boutique-hotel photography. Large upright sans-serif type feels direct and practical. Broad blue surfaces, clear day columns, and uninterrupted horizontal space recall planning across a week without imitating a spreadsheet throughout the page.

Palette: paper #F7F9FC, ink #182741, blue #294ED1, blue wash #E9EEFF, rest mint #DAEBDD, white #FFFFFF. Type: Manrope for headings and body, with weight and scale carrying hierarchy. Alignment: left aligned throughout; numerals align in schedule cells.

## Plan critique

A generic blue SaaS hero would be interchangeable. Ground the opening in the actual scheduling trade-off: scattered breaks versus consecutive days off. Use a complete illustrated week, visible names, and an explicit “illustration, not a live solver” label. Product tour uses interactive coded product views with fictional data (`ProductIllustration.tsx`). Avoid manufactured social proof. Preserve the wave mark as the brand anchor while replacing the page's composition, palette and typography.
